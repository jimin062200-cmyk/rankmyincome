Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. RankMyIncome is designed to work without user accounts and without storing the income amount you enter in a RankMyIncome database.

Calculator inputs

The country and annual income you enter are processed in your browser to calculate a result. RankMyIncome does not require an account, and the calculator does not send your entered income to a RankMyIncome application database for the purpose of calculating your percentile.

Sharing results

If you use the share or copy-result feature, RankMyIncome generates text containing your country, tier, and estimated top percentage. The income amount you entered is not included in the generated share text.

Server and hosting logs

The hosting or infrastructure provider may automatically process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time, and requested pages for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and operational logging.

Advertising and cookies

RankMyIncome has installed the Google AdSense script while the site is being connected to and reviewed by Google AdSense. Ads may not yet be displayed. Google and its advertising technologies may process technical information such as device, browser, network, and request information and may use cookies or similar technologies where applicable. Advertising behavior and disclosures may change when ad serving is enabled.

Consent

Where advertising or analytics technologies require consent under applicable law or platform policy, RankMyIncome will use an appropriate consent mechanism before serving advertising that requires such consent.

Analytics

RankMyIncome does not currently use a separate third-party analytics provider. If analytics is added later, this policy will be updated to identify the provider and the categories of information it processes.

Data source

The calculator uses statistical distribution data from WID.world. These are aggregate statistical datasets and are separate from the personal income value you type into the calculator.

Children

RankMyIncome is a general informational calculator and is not designed to create personal profiles of children. No account creation is required.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when hosting, advertising, analytics, consent tools, or other site features change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
