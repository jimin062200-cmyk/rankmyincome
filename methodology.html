Methodology

RankMyIncome estimates where an annual income sits within a national and global distribution. The public calculator only switches out of Preview mode when its active dataset has been verified and cleared for the intended use.

Income concept

The planned WID.world implementation uses tptinc992j: the threshold of pre-tax national income for adults aged 20 and over using the equal-split-adult population concept. In plain language, the input remains approximate annual pre-tax income, but the statistical concept is broader than salary alone. The active source and data year are shown with each result.

Country percentile

The calculator compares the entered local-currency income with percentile thresholds for that country. Between two published thresholds, RankMyIncome uses logarithmic interpolation because income distributions are strongly skewed. Results are estimates rather than exact measurements.

Worldwide percentile

For the planned WID.world implementation, local income is converted with xlceup999i, the local-currency-per-EUR-PPP conversion used by the official 2026 comparator code, then compared with the WID world income thresholds. This accounts for purchasing-power differences rather than relying only on market exchange rates.

Tier system

The tier names are created by RankMyIncome for presentation and entertainment. They are not official classifications. Challenger begins at the top 0.1%, Grandmaster at top 1%, Master at top 5%, Diamond at top 10%, Emerald at top 15%, Platinum at top 25%, Gold at top 40%, Silver at top 60%, Bronze at top 80%, and Iron below that.

Data source and reuse

RankMyIncome keeps statistical data separate from application code. WID.world is the planned source because its distributional income data match the calculator’s intended concept. Current World Inequality Lab legal material states that open data available on its website can be used without prior authorization. Before advertising is enabled, RankMyIncome will still verify the current reuse and attribution terms and list the exact production dataset here. See WID Codes Dictionary and WID Methodology.

Data limitations

Income-distribution estimates can vary with survey coverage, tax data, national accounts, interpolation, extrapolation, population definitions, reference years, and other methodological choices. Country coverage may differ. Results should be treated as approximate comparisons, not financial or tax advice.

Independence

RankMyIncome is an independent calculator and is not operated by or endorsed by any statistical provider unless explicitly stated.
