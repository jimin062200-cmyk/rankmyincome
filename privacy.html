Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026. This page describes the intended privacy behavior of RankMyIncome before advertising is enabled.

Calculator inputs

RankMyIncome does not require an account. The calculator is designed to process the country and income you enter locally in your browser. The site does not need to store your entered income in a user account or database to calculate a result.

Server and hosting logs

The hosting provider may automatically process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and requested pages for security, reliability, and operational logging.

Advertising and cookies

When advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers such as Google may use cookies, local storage, device information, or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads. This policy will be updated with the final advertising providers before public monetization begins.

Consent

Where required, the site will display a consent mechanism before using advertising or analytics technologies that require consent. For Google advertising in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the production site will use an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform where required.

Analytics

No analytics provider is assumed by this preview policy. If analytics is added, this page will identify the provider and the categories of data it processes.

Children

RankMyIncome is a general informational calculator and is not designed to collect personal profiles from children. No account creation is required.

Changes

This policy may change when the final hosting, advertising, analytics, or consent tools are connected. The updated date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

A contact email will be added before public launch.
