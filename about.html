About RankMyIncome

RankMyIncome is an independent income-percentile calculator that turns statistical income-distribution data into an easy country rank, worldwide rank, and game-style tier.

What the site does

Enter an approximate annual pre-tax income and choose a country. RankMyIncome estimates where that amount sits within the selected country's income distribution and within a worldwide distribution adjusted for purchasing power.

Where the data comes from

The production calculator uses income-distribution and purchasing-power data from WID.world. The current dataset covers 208 countries and territories. Country pages show the income-distribution year and selected threshold values used by the calculator.

What a result means

Results are statistical estimates, not exact salary rankings. The underlying WID income concept is broader than salary alone, and RankMyIncome interpolates between published threshold points. Values outside the available threshold range require extrapolation and carry more uncertainty.

Privacy by design

No account is required. The calculator is designed to process the country and income amount in the browser, and RankMyIncome does not maintain an application database of the income amount entered for a calculation. See the Privacy Policy for hosting, advertising, cookie, and consent information.

Advertising

RankMyIncome does not currently display third-party advertising. If advertising is enabled, the site will identify the provider, update its privacy disclosures, and use consent controls where required.

Independence

RankMyIncome is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by WID.world. Tier names such as Master, Grandmaster, and Challenger are RankMyIncome presentation labels and are not official statistical classifications.

For the calculation details, data concepts, interpolation rules, and limitations, read the Methodology.
